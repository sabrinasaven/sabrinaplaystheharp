Biography

Born in London, Sabrina began her studies on the harp at the age of 11 alongside piano and jazz clarinet. Her formative training began under the tutelage of Valeria Clarke and later Daphne Boden. It was not long before she was performing publicly, playing at school assemblies and accompanying school productions on the harp. Her early musical life also extended into theatre: she gave solo and improvised performances with the Russian theatre group 'Goodge Street,' work that was recognised in 2017 with a Stars of the Albion Diploma of Participation for her musical contribution to the production Prediction.

Sabrina briefly studied at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama's Junior Department, where she studied with Ruth Faber. She was then offered a place at Chetham's School of Music in Manchester for her sixth-form studies, taking first study harp with Marie Leenhardt and second study piano with Jonathan Middleton. Her time at Chetham's broadened her musicianship considerably: she pursued extracurricular studies in jazz improvisation, studied advanced chamber playing, and performed with the Chetham's Symphony Orchestra and Symphonic Wind Orchestra under conductors including Ben Gernon and Paul Mann at the Bridgewater Hall and Stoller Hall in Manchester, alongside further orchestral engagements with Chetham's Big Band conducted by Mike Lovatt and provided harp accompaniment for the Chetham's Choir at Manchester Cathedral.

Sabrina completed her undergraduate studies at the Guildhall School of Music and Drama, one of the UK's leading conservatoires, graduating in 2025 with a BMus (Hons) in Classical Harp (2:1) under Keziah Thomas. Alongside her core studies, she took a wide range of electives spanning music history, workshop skills, music therapy, jazz and extended jazz techniques, early harp studies, harp-focused contemporary studies, orchestral coaching, and opera.

This breadth was matched by an active performing schedule: she played with the Guildhall Symphony Orchestra at the Barbican Hall under distinguished conductors, including Takuo Yuasa, Antony Hermus and Alpesh Chauhan, and with the Guildhall Symphonic Wind Orchestra under Beth Randell and Fraser MacAulay, as well as with the King's College London Symphony Orchestra.

Her orchestral development has been shaped through coaching with some of the profession's most established harpists, including Bryn Lewis of the London Symphony Orchestra, Helen Tunstall of the London Sinfonietta, and Marie Leenhardt of the Hallé Orchestra. She has further supplemented her training with an extensive series of masterclasses, working with leading international harpists such as Sivan Magen, Isabelle Perrin, Masumi Nagasawa, Catriona McKay, and Park Stickney, among others, covering repertoire from Debussy to contemporary, jazz, and folk styles.

On the operatic stage, she has performed with Guildhall Opera at the Silk Street Theatre under conductor Dominic Wheeler, and with Guildhall Opera Makers - the school's dedicated platform for premiering new operatic works by emerging composers, and more recently with the Bedfordshire Youth Opera under Richard Sisson.

Beyond the concert platform, Sabrina has built a varied portfolio as a chamber musician and community performer. Since 2020, she has been performing regularly in a range of chamber combinations, most notably her duo for saxophone and harp. She has given premieres of new works for harp and for voice and harp, including at the Guildhall Harp Festival in 2024, and maintains a regular presence in community and outreach settings, including performances at St Olave's Church and the Royal London Hospital, a workshop placement with the Shoreditch Trust Stroke Project, and a solo performance for the Russian Embassy.

From September 2024 to July 2025, she served as Assistant to the Guildhall Head of Harp and Administrator to the GSMD Harp Department, and she has run her own private teaching practice since 2022. Sabrina continues to perform widely as an orchestral, chamber, and solo musician across the UK.
